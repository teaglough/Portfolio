About

I am currently a third-year Mechanical Engineering student at Michigan State University in East Lansing, Michigan, on track to graduate in Spring 2028. I have always enjoyed creating, building, and figuring out how things work, which ultimately led me to pursue mechanical engineering. I especially enjoy using CAD and 3D printing to turn ideas into physical designs, whether I am working on a new project, solving a problem, or finding a way to fix something that is broken.

I also serve as the Treasurer for Keep State Green, a student organization dedicated to keeping Michigan State's campus clean through organized cleanup efforts. These experiences have strengthened my ability to collaborate, communicate, take on responsibility, and work toward a common goal as part of a team.

I enjoy hands-on projects where I can combine creativity with technical problem-solving, and I am always looking for opportunities to learn new skills and challenge myself. I am currently seeking a Summer 2027 mechanical engineering internship where I can apply the skills I have developed through my coursework, personal projects, and work experience while continuing to grow as an engineer.
